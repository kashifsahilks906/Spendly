Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data when you use our personal expense tracking service.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense records you log — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to help keep the service secure and reliable.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve the service: authenticating your account, displaying your expense history, generating summaries and spending insights, and communicating with you about your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and protected with reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting and infrastructure, who process data only as necessary to provide those services and are bound to keep it confidential. We do not share your expense data with advertisers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
